About

Meet Saroj Joshi

Saroj Joshi is a multiple award-winning cookbook author and jeweler. She began conducting cooking classes in 1985, and her mastery of creating vegetarian dishes inspired by international cuisine has produced three sold-out books.

Her third cookbook, Unique Book of Vegetarian Cooking, was launched by Crosswords Bookstores across four cities in India and went on to win the prestigious Gourmand World Cook Book Award for Best Vegetarian Cuisine Book in India — and was nominated for the World's Best Vegetarian Cuisine Book in Paris, France.

Available at Crosswords in India, Kitchen Arts & Letters in New York City, and worldwide via Amazon. Saroj is currently working on her next book featuring vegan alkaline recipes.
